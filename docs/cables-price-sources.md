## Pricing rule (updated 17 Oct 2026)
Our price = **average of the prices found for the product at retailers worldwide**, converted to AUD inc GST, minus 5%. Prices of $20 or more are rounded down to the dollar; under $20 to the cent.
Conversion: 1 USD = 1.443, 1 EUR = 1.621, 1 GBP = 1.907, 1 NZD = 0.91, 1 SGD = 1.12, 1 CAD = 1.05 AUD, ¥110 = 1 AUD, ₹1 = 0.0172 AUD, 1 DKK = 0.217 AUD. Local VAT/GST is removed from tax-inclusive prices, then 10% GST is added. Sources marked "comparable" are the same type of cable from another brand, used where the exact item had no listing (mainly Titan). Sources marked "estimate" are scaled from another length or pack of the same product. Listings for a different length, pack or colour are noted in brackets.
