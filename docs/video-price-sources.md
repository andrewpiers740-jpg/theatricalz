## Pricing rule
Our price = **average of the prices found for the product at retailers worldwide**, converted to AUD inc GST, minus 5%. Prices of $20 or more are rounded down to the dollar.
Conversion: 1 USD = 1.443, 1 EUR = 1.621, 1 GBP = 1.907, 1 NZD = 0.91, 1 SGD = 1.12, 1 CAD = 1.05, ¥110 = 1 AUD, ₹1 = 0.0172, 1 DKK = 0.217, 1 CZK = 0.0648, 1 NOK = 0.14 AUD. Local VAT/GST is removed from tax-inclusive prices (EUR/GBP 20%, NZD 15%, SGD 9%, JPY 10%, INR 18%, DKK/NOK 25%, CZK 21%), then 10% GST is added; US and Canadian prices are pre-tax. Listings include list (RRP) prices, current retailer prices and sale prices where those were all that could be found — each is labelled. Hot Rod Cameras' Canadian storefront (en-ca) prices are treated as CAD.
